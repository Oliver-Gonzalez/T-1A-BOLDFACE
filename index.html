T-1A Boldface Emergency Procedures and Operating Limitations 8 Oct 25
Section 1. Boldface Emergency Procedures
Engine Fire During Ground Operations
Correct Answer:THROTTLES - CUTOFF
Engine Fire During Flight
If engine fire switchlight remains illuminated, L or R FIRE DET FAIL illuminates,
fire warning test fails, or fire conditions persist:
Correct Answer:THROTTLE - IDLE (If conditions persist:) THROTTLE - CUTOFF ENGINE FIRE SWITCH - PUSH BOTTLE ARMED SWITCH - PUSH
Smoke Or Fumes In Aircraft
Correct Answer:OXYGEN MASKS - DON / 100%
Pitch Trim/Roll Trim/Rudder Trim Runaway
Correct Answer:TRIM INT & A/P DISENGAGE SWITCH - PUSH AND HOLD
Low Cabin Pressure
Correct Answer:OXYGEN MASKS - DON / 100%

Notes:

(1) Time limit starts when throttles first advanced for takeoff thrust.

(2) Normal Flight: Normal oil pressure is 60 to 83 PSI at N₂ speeds above 60 %. Oil pressure below 60 PSI is undesirable and should be tolerated only for the safe recovery of the flight, preferably at reduced power setting. Oil pressure below 40 PSI is unsafe.

(3) Under cold starting conditions, oil pressure between 83 PSI and 95 PSI is limited to 90 seconds.

(4) The ITT during ground starts should normally not exceed 550 °C.
